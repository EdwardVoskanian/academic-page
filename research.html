Research

Research Interests

My research lies broadly in analysis and connects fractal geometry, complex dimensions, mathematical diffraction, quasicrystals, Diophantine approximation, and computational experimentation.

Fractal Geometry

Fractal strings, complex dimensions, geometric zeta functions, and oscillatory phenomena.

Mathematical Diffraction

Autocorrelation, diffraction measures, lattice and nonlattice patterns, and quasicrystalline structure.

Computation

Numerical approximations, visualization, lattice approximations, and algorithms for quasiperiodic patterns.
