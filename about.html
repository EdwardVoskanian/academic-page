About

Hi there! My name is Eddie, and I teach high school mathematics at Campbell Hall Episcopal. I grew up in Southern California, and my interest in mathematics began while I was a community college student studying engineering. After taking what felt like countless math courses, I found an incredible community of mathematics students and professors that completely changed my career path. I eventually earned my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of California, Riverside. My research is at the intersection of fractal geometry and aperiodic order, and I enjoy involving students in mathematical research whenever I can. Whether it's an original research project or an expository topic, I love helping students experience what it's like to explore mathematics beyond the classroom. When I'm not teaching, you'll probably find me making sushi for family and friends, tending to my offset smoker for a Central Texas–style barbecue, playing guitar, or playing Street Fighter, a game I've enjoyed since I was about nine years old.

Biography

I am a mathematician, educator, mentor, and musician. My mathematical work focuses on analysis, fractal geometry, complex dimensions, mathematical diffraction, and computational methods.

My teaching is guided by a growth mindset and a commitment to making challenging mathematics accessible through applications, visualization, discussion, active learning, and technology.

I currently serve as Secondary Mathematics Faculty at Campbell Hall Episcopal, where I teach mathematics, mentor students, support mathematical enrichment and competition activities, and participate in music-related student projects.
