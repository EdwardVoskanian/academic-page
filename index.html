Welcome

I am a mathematician and educator whose work brings together analysis, fractal geometry, mathematical diffraction, number theory, computation, and undergraduate research.

My teaching emphasizes conceptual understanding, visualization, applications, active learning, and the thoughtful use of computational tools. Outside mathematics, I am also involved in music and collaborative musical work with students.
